Hospitality · Since 1969

Hospitality is where our story began.

In 1969, Sardar Darshan Singh Chawla founded Wanton House on Hill Road, Bandra. More than five decades later, hospitality remains at the heart of Wanton Group through our restaurants and hotels.

Restaurants

Wanton House

A Bandra institution, built over generations.

Wanton House is the foundation of Wanton Group. Established on Hill Road, Bandra West, in 1969, the restaurant has been part of the family's journey from its very beginning. Its food, recipes and hospitality became the starting point for a business that would eventually grow beyond the restaurant.
