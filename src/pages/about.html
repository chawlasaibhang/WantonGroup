Our story · Since 1969

Built by one generation. Carried forward by three.

Wanton Group was founded in 1969 by Sardar Darshan Singh Chawla, whose vision and values became the foundation of the family business. Those values were passed to his son, D.S. Chawla, and continue to guide the third generation today.

In 1969, Sardar Darshan Singh Chawla founded Wanton House on Hill Road, Bandra West, Mumbai. What began with a restaurant became the foundation of a family business that would grow into hospitality, food products and lifestyle.

The business has evolved over three generations, but its foundation remains the same: the values instilled by its founder and carried forward by his family.
