**WANTON GROUP**

*Website content & brand direction*

# 1. Website purpose

Build a premium, warm and heritage-led website for Wanton Group, a
third-generation family-run business based in Bandra, Mumbai. The
website should connect three businesses under one family story:
Hospitality, FMCG and Lifestyle.

# 2. Core brand story

Wanton Group was founded in 1969 by Sardar Darshan Singh Chawla, who
established Wanton House on Hill Road, Bandra West, Mumbai. His vision
and values became the foundation of the family business. Those values
were passed to his son, D.S. Chawla, the owner and guiding force behind
the group\'s vision, and continue through the third generation.

The group has grown from its original restaurant into hospitality, food
products and lifestyle. The story is not about being a large
conglomerate. It is about one family, one long-standing business, and
different ventures that have grown naturally from what the family knows
and does.

# 3. Brand positioning

Primary brand line: "Since 1969. Three generations. One family vision."

Supporting positioning: "From one restaurant to a family of businesses."

The three verticals should feel connected: Wanton House is where the
story began; WHS Sauces carries restaurant recipes beyond the
restaurant; Ajuni Luxe extends the family\'s business into gifting and
lifestyle.

# 4. Tone and writing rules

-   Warm, direct, specific and understated.

-   Use short sentences and active voice.

-   Let facts, dates, streets and family names create credibility.

-   Use sentence case for headings.

-   No exclamation marks.

-   Avoid corporate filler and generic language.

-   Do not use: leverage, seamless, unlock, empower, simply, just,
    successfully.

-   Calls to action must be verb-first: "Explore hospitality", "Discover
    our story", "Enquire about a franchise".

-   Do not invent awards, scale, founding details, expansion dates,
    hotel details, sourcing claims or product claims.

# 6. Visual direction for the website AI

-   Design for quiet luxury hospitality, not a generic corporate group.

-   Use generous whitespace, restrained typography and strong editorial
    hierarchy.

-   The heritage should feel authentic rather than nostalgic or themed.

-   Use the 1969 origin as a recurring visual anchor.

-   Photography should prioritise real family/business/restaurant
    imagery where available.

-   Avoid excessive gradients, glossy effects, stock-corporate imagery,
    crowded cards and generic corporate illustrations.

-   The three verticals should feel related but have enough visual
    distinction to be memorable.

-   Keep navigation simple and premium. Prioritise readability on
    mobile.

-   Use subtle references to Mumbai, Bandra and the family\'s heritage
    rather than decorative clichés.

-   Do not fabricate imagery, testimonials, statistics, awards or
    historical details.

# 7. Content guardrails

Do not add factual claims that are not supplied in this document. If the
website builder needs missing information (hotel names, hotel features,
WHS product range, sourcing details, founder biography beyond the
supplied facts, awards, dates or statistics), flag it as a content
requirement rather than inventing it.
