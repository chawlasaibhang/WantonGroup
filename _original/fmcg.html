The first bottle: Original Schezwan Sauce

Our first release carries a recipe with real history — the Schezwan sauce developed and served at Wanton House for over five decades, now crafted in small batches and bottled for home and professional kitchens.

Every jar carries the same heritage seal as the restaurant it came from: made in small batches, true to the original recipe.

A restaurant recipe with a different purpose

We started with a restaurant. The sauces came from the food we served there, the recipes we developed, and the flavours that became part of the Wanton House experience. WHS Sauces takes those recipes and puts them into a bottle, making them available beyond our own kitchen — for everyday cooking at home and for professional foodservice use alike.
